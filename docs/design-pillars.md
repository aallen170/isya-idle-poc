# Design Pillars

This document defines the core design principles that guide development of the Idle MMO project.  
All major gameplay systems should align with these pillars.

---

## 1. Solo-Complete MMO Experience

Players can experience the full dungeon and progression loop without relying on other players.

The game should avoid traditional MMO bottlenecks like waiting for tanks, healers, or groups.  
All core content must be playable by a single player managing their own party.

---

## 2. Multi-Character Party Management

Players control a party of characters simultaneously, filling traditional MMO roles such as tank, healer, support, and DPS.

Party composition, character builds, and synergy become a central strategic layer of gameplay.

---

## 3. Strategy Over Mechanical Input

Gameplay emphasizes planning, preparation, and optimization rather than fast manual inputs.

Players design ability routines, builds, and compositions that execute automatically during combat.

---

## 4. Idle-First Progression

Characters continue progressing through automated systems while the player is offline or focusing on other activities.

The game supports long-term progression without requiring constant active play.

---

## 5. Respect Player Time

Players should always feel like progress is being made.

Systems should avoid excessive waiting, group-finding barriers, or grind loops that stall advancement.

---

## 6. Accessible & Transparent Progression

Players should always understand:

- how to become stronger
- where their power comes from
- what they should do next

Systems should avoid hidden mechanics or confusing progression paths.

---

## 7. Flexible Play Sessions

Players should be able to meaningfully progress in both:

- short sessions (checking routines, crafting, progression)
- longer sessions (dungeons, optimization, build tuning)

The game supports both active and passive playstyles.

---

## 8. Optional Mechanical Proficiency

Players can choose to partake in optional challenges that disable the ability routine & auto-casting features, forcing players to manage their team and ability rotations manually. This will test the limits of their ability and resource management skills.
